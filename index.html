Before you buy

A few practical answers.

What fields are included?

Every list includes full name, job title, verified work email, company name, website, LinkedIn profile URL, headcount band, agency niche, and location (city/state).

How is the list delivered?

As a CSV file sent to your email within 48 hours of your order. You can sort, filter, or import it into your outreach tool.

Can I request a different audience?

These lists cover US marketing agencies with 5-50 employees only. If you need a different segment, email me first and I’ll tell you honestly whether I can build it.

Do you guarantee replies or sales?

No. I guarantee the list criteria, the fields, the verification process, and the 14-day bounce replacement. Results from outreach depend on your offer and messaging.

How should I use the data?

Use it for one-to-one business outreach in line with applicable laws and your email provider’s rules. Don’t resell the list or add people to bulk sequences without consent where required.
